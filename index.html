Click anywhere in the text to make changes. Select text and use B, I, or U to format it. A monospaced font preserves the spacing between chords and lyrics.

Dashed boundaries are calculated from the same Letter or A4 page boxes used to print. They update as you edit.

Drag chords from the organizer, sticker tray, or printable CHORDS bank onto any page. Each drop adds a copy. Shift+click duplicates a sticker; Ctrl+Shift+drag snaps to the shared 5px grid. What you see on each paper page is what prints.
